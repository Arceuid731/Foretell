# Source-based review checkpoints

Selected before inspecting generated advice, from the complete archived wiki and workbook text. These are targeted semantic checks, not an exhaustive accuracy benchmark. Assess incorrect extra advice as well as omissions. Counts of generated mechanics are not scores. An unprepared/rejected guide is unavailable, not automatically correct.

## Palace 31–40
- Ixtab is the sole floor boss; ordinary floor enemies must not become the boss roster.
- Drop lingering AoEs near the arena edges.
- Kill adds before Scream/Prey makes their Tornado lethal.
- Tornado belongs to the adds, not an invented Ixtab cast.
- Shadow Flare is unavoidable heavy raid damage, not an AoE to dodge.

## Haukke Manor
- Three boss encounters: Claviger; the Jester/Steward pair; Amandine. Named actors may be split for matching, but must keep mechanic ownership.
- Claviger: frontal Sweet Steel, interrupt Void Fire II, stun/avoid Dark Mist.
- Jester: interrupt Ice Spikes. Steward: stun/avoid Soul Drain. Kill Jester first.
- Amandine: look away from Manor Sentry; after Seduce releases, look away again.
- Kill Lady's Handmaiden to prevent Stoneskin; preserve the add's identity and action ownership.
- Preserve Void Fire III / Void Thunder III and the tankbuster distinction.
- Do not silently recommend obsolete lamps/bomb adds from the workbook over the explicitly reworked 6.1 encounter.
- Workbook row 15: E2 First Boss, G2 Second Boss (pair), I2 Third Boss (fleeing Sentry), K2 Fourth Boss (old Amandine). Do not map column ordinal blindly onto the three current boss encounters.

## Fractal Continuum Hard
- Motherbit, The Ultima Warrior, The Ultima Beast, in this order. No bosses from other workbook rows.
- Motherbit Citadel Buster: get behind the line early, before the dividing barrier prevents crossing.
- Warrior Citadel Buster: dodge sideways from a frontal line, not the Motherbit half-arena instruction.
- Sophia: marked white/black players stack together.
- Zurvan: fire debuff to fire meteor, ice to ice; do not reverse.
- Beast: raised claw Death Spin -> out; standing on hind legs Aether Bend -> in. These have no castbar.
- Distinguish Motherbit's lingering Allagan Gravity puddle from Beast's markers on all players.
- Demi Ultima: ordinary raid damage versus sub-10%-HP long-cast enrage; preserve the condition instead of inventing a separate cast name.
- Workbook D12/F12/H12 must map respectively to Motherbit/Warrior/Beast, retaining its posture cues.

## Labyrinth of the Ancients
- Four main bosses Bone Dragon, Thanatos, King Behemoth, Phlegethon; Atomos/Allagan Bomb/Vassago are legitimate intervening encounters. No Syrcus/World of Darkness roster or mechanics.
- Bone Dragon: separated skeletons, kill before reaching corpse; tank location is a regional convention, not a universal requirement.
- Atomos: four players remain on each back platform to unlock another group's Atomos.
- Thanatos: Astral Realignment holders attack boss; others protect pots/kill adds.
- Bomb encounter: Napalm first, Balloon next; three Vassagos protect the central bomb.
- King Behemoth: comets outside targeting ring; protect them from Iron Giants; hide behind a comet from Ecliptic Meteor.
- Phlegethon: Ancient Flare -> assigned platform, at least four for barrier; preserve second-cast urgency.
- Workbook F43 Ancient Flare (platform barrier) must not receive F54 Scylla's puddle instruction.
